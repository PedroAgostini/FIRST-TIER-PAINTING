Questions Homeowners Ask Us.

How fast can I get an estimate?

Send the form or a text with a few photos. We reply quickly, set up a visit that fits your schedule and send the estimate by text with the scope and price spelled out.

Can you paint exteriors during a Massachusetts winter?

Exterior paint needs dry wood and mild temperatures to cure, so exterior work runs from spring to fall. In winter we focus on interiors, cabinets and commercial spaces, and we can book your exterior for the first good weeks of spring.

Do I need to move furniture or empty the room?

No. We move what needs moving, cover floors and furniture, and put the room back together when we finish. Just set aside anything fragile or personal.

What else do you paint besides walls and siding?

Doors, stairs, windows, gutters, furniture and spray finishes. We also do post construction cleaning and the small repairs a good paint job needs.

Do you install wallpaper or hang drywall?

No. We do not install wallpaper or hang full drywall. We do patch, fill and sand the spots that need it before painting.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Worcester and work across Central Massachusetts. Put your ZIP code in the form and we will confirm your area when we reply.
