# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. GSAP + ScrollTrigger and Three.js from CDN. Estimate form posts to a configurable Formspree endpoint that emails Info@firsttierpainting.com.

## Users

Homeowners in Worcester and Central Massachusetts, mostly American, arriving from Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram) on a phone, later from Google Ads and Google Local Services. They have faded siding, peeling trim, scuffed walls or tired cabinets and want a painter who shows up, keeps the house clean and quotes fast. Secondary: small commercial owners (offices, stores).

## Product Purpose

One page site for First Tier Painting, a painting contractor based in Worcester, MA. Its job is to turn paid traffic into estimate requests (form, text message, call). Success is qualified leads with a ZIP code inside the service area.

## Positioning

Owner operated: the people who quote the job are the people who paint it. Built around New England weather (moisture, freeze, moss). Punctual, flexible scheduling, clean work areas, long term relationship with the homeowner.

## Product Principles

1. Every section ends one tap away from an estimate; texting is the easiest path.
2. Speak to the homeowner's problem (weather damage, mess, no shows) before the service.
3. Only claim what is true today; leave proof slots ready for real evidence.
4. Fast on a phone over a cellular connection; ad traffic does not wait.
